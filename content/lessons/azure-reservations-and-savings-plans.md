> **In one line:** Commit to 1-year or 3-year compute usage to unlock up to 72% discounts compared to Pay-As-You-Go pricing.

## The idea

Default Pay-As-You-Go (PAYG) cloud pricing offers maximum agility and zero commitment, but it carries the highest per-hour rates. For steady-state production workloads that run 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, paying hourly retail rates leads to massive unnecessary expenditure.

Microsoft provides two primary long-term financial commitment discount models:

1. **Azure Reserved Instances (Reservations):**
   - You commit to a specific resource type, SKU family, and region for **1 or 3 years** (e.g., 4 instances of Standard_D4s_v5 in East US).
   - Delivers up to **72% savings** compared to Pay-As-You-Go pricing.
   - Applies to Virtual Machines, Azure SQL Databases, Cosmos DB throughput, Synapse, and Azure Storage reserved capacity.
   - Highly cost-effective for fixed, stable architectures that do not change instance sizes or regions.

2. **Azure Savings Plans for Compute:**
   - You commit to a fixed hourly compute spend (e.g., \$20/hour) for **1 or 3 years**.
   - Delivers up to **65% savings** across compute workloads.
   - **Extreme flexibility:** Automatically applies discounts across Virtual Machines, Azure App Service, Azure Functions premium, and Azure Container Apps regardless of VM family, operating system, or geographical region.

| Feature | Pay-As-You-Go | Azure Savings Plan | Azure Reservations |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Commitment** | None (Hourly) | 1 or 3 years (\$ spend/hr) | **1 or 3 years (Specific SKU)** |
| **Discount Potential** | 0% (List Price) | **Up to 65%** | **Up to 72%** |
| **Region Flexibility** | Complete | **Any global region** | Locked to specific region |
| **Compute Flexibility** | Complete | **Any VM family / App Service** | Locked to specific VM family |
| **Payment Options** | Monthly | Monthly or All Upfront | Monthly or All Upfront |

> **Tip:** Stack **Azure Hybrid Benefit (AHB)** on top of Azure Reservations for Windows Server and SQL Server to achieve combined discounts exceeding 80% compared to standard Pay-As-You-Go rates.

## See it

Inspect reservation recommendations generated by Azure Cost Management based on historic consumption telemetry:

```bash
# Query automated reservation recommendations for Virtual Machines
az consumption reservation recommendation list   --look-back-period "Last30Days"   --term "P1Y"   --output table

# Review active reservation commitments and expiration schedules
az reservations reservation list   --output table
```

Azure analyzes your trailing 30 days of 24/7 compute consumption and calculates the exact SKU quantities where reservations will immediately lower operational costs.

## Go deeper

Organizations adopting FinOps practices typically employ a **blended commitment strategy**:
- Baseline steady-state VMs and production SQL databases are covered by **Azure Reservations** for maximum discount (up to 72%).
- Dynamic, evolving workloads across containers and App Services are covered by **Azure Savings Plans** for flexibility (up to 65%).
- Unpredictable burst capacity and ephemeral CI/CD runners remain on **Pay-As-You-Go** or **Spot instances**.

## Watch out

- Reservations are billed for every hour of the commitment term regardless of whether the virtual machines are powered on or deallocated.
- Azure Savings Plans prioritize hourly consumption automatically; unspent commitment within an hour does not roll over to future hours.

## Remember

- Azure Reservations offer up to 72% discounts on dedicated SKUs and regions in exchange for 1 or 3-year commitments.
- Azure Savings Plans provide up to 65% savings with automatic flexibility across VM families, regions, and serverless compute.
- Combine commitments with Azure Hybrid Benefit to maximize total cost reduction on steady-state enterprise workloads.
